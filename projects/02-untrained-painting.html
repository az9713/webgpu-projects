An untrained neural network as a painting machine

Every pixel's coordinates go into a small network with random weights that nobody trained. The three outputs are read as red, green and blue. Re-roll the seed for a different painting. The first layer is a million-row matrix multiply at 1024×1024, and it is a thin, wide shape, so it does far less arithmetic per row than a square one.

Input per pixel: x, y in [−1, 1] and the distance from the centre. Layer 1 is Sin of a random projection, scaled by the frequency slider. The hidden layers are MatMul, Add, Tanh. The output is Sigmoid. Everything after the coordinate upload stays on the card until the final colour readback.

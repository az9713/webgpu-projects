The science

The wave function obeys the Schrödinger equation i ∂ψ/∂t = −½∇2ψ + Vψ, in units where ℏ = m = 1. The two terms fight each other: the kinetic part is simple in frequency space, where it is a multiplication by k2/2, and the potential part is simple in position space, where it is a multiplication by V. Neither is simple in the other. The split-step method takes the short step anyway, applying each part where it is cheap:

ψ ← exp(−iVΔt) · ψ, then ψ ← F−1[ exp(−ik2Δt/2) · Fψ ].

Every factor has modulus one, so the step cannot create or destroy probability. That is why the total staying at 1.0000 is a real check and not a formality. The splitting is not exact, because the two operators do not commute; the error per step is O(Δt2), which is why Δt is kept at 0.4.

Tunnelling is the part with no classical counterpart. Inside a barrier higher than the particle's energy the wave number turns imaginary, κ = √(2(V0 − E)), and the wave decays as e−κx instead of oscillating. It is small on the far side, not zero. For a square barrier of width a the exact transmission is T = [ 1 + V02 sinh2(κa) / (4E(V0−E)) ]−1. The default barrier is 4 cells wide, V0 = 0.6 against a packet energy of about 0.405, which gives κ = 0.62 and T = 2.4%. The page measures 2.3%. Read that figure while probability still in the box is still 1, at about step 280: the packet has finished crossing the barrier but nothing has reached the absorbing edges yet. Later the fraction is computed on a vanishing remainder and is noise.

The split-step method alternates a multiply in position space with a multiply in frequency space, so it is a DFT sandwich. Each step: multiply by exp(−iVΔt), transform, multiply by exp(−ik2Δt/2), transform back. A complex multiply on interleaved (real, imaginary) pairs is Mul, a Gather that swaps the pair, Mul and Add. The two-dimensional transform is DFT along one axis, Transpose, DFT, Transpose, because the fast path runs along one axis only. The phase tables come from Cos, Sin, Neg and Concat when the barrier changes. Units: ℏ = m = 1, one grid cell = 1, Δt = 0.4. The left and right edges carry a smooth absorbing mask, one more Mul, so the reflected part does not wrap round the periodic box and arrive on the far side. Total probability is read back every frame: it stays at 1.0000 until something reaches an edge, which is the check that the transform pair is unitary.

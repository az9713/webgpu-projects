Eleven WebGPU kernel projects

Each page is one self-contained HTML file. It loads @huggingface/kernels@preview from jsDelivr, runs on the GPU in this tab, and needs no npm, no bundler and no build step. Open this index over http://, not file:// — the module import fails on a file URL.

The result under each title was measured once on an RTX 3050. Read every number as ±10%.

  • Catalogue 16 · score 91
    A quantum wavepacket tunnelling through a barrier

    256² grid, 17 calls a step, 109 steps/s. Probability holds at 1.0000 until the edges absorb. A barrier of 0.6 against energy 0.405 lets 2.3% through.

  • Catalogue 22 · score 89
    An untrained neural network as a painting machine

    Random weights, no training, one readback. 512² in ~380 ms, 1024² in ~620 ms over 21 calls.

  • Catalogue 36 · score 89
    Quantisation error microscope

    Max error 0.035 at int8, 0.63 at 4-bit, 4.05 at 2-bit with a single scale. Per-row scales are selectable. 4-bit and 2-bit are simulated inside int8.

  • Catalogue 81 · score 89
    Poison the training set with a click

    Logistic regression, 8 calls a step, ~96 steps/s. Ten poisoned points tilt the line and halve the weights; clean accuracy still holds near 98%.

  • Catalogue 111 · score 89
    The cost of coming back to JavaScript

    A chain of eight calls, two ways. Keeping the buffer on the card is 3.0 to 4.6× faster at N ≥ 256.

  • Catalogue 76 · score 87
    Train a digit classifier in the tab, then draw a digit

    Fetches the 10.7 MB tfjs MNIST sprite. 784→64→10, batch 128, lr 0.3: 94.8% test accuracy after ~8 epochs at 80–92 steps/s.

  • Catalogue 77 · score 86
    Two moons, one hidden layer, gradients by hand

    2→16→1, 26 calls a step, ~100 steps/s, 99.5% at 630 steps. The ReLU gradient is a Greater and a Where.

  • Catalogue 70 · score 86
    Principal components without a decomposition kernel

    Power iteration on a 450×6 set: 27 iterations at an eigenvalue gap of 2.0, 88 at a gap of 1.1. Residual |Cv − λv| ≈ 1e-5.

  • Catalogue 90 · score 85
    Take a floating point number apart

    BitCast, BitShift and BitwiseAnd decode the sign, exponent and mantissa. exp(x) overflows at x ≥ 88.73, and this GPU flushes subnormals to zero.

  • Catalogue 115 · score 85
    Slime mould: a million agents that build networks

    250k agents on 512², 46 calls a step, 44 steps/s. A web forms by step ~140, then coarsens into thick veins.

  • Catalogue 104 · score 85
    Why the stable form of softmax exists

    The sum overflows at 88.2645 and exp at 88.7228. Between them the naive panel returns eight zeros and lights no red. The stable form holds to 5.9e-8 from −120 to 120.

Slime mould: a million agents that build networks

Each agent moves, leaves a trail, and steers toward the strongest trail ahead of it. Within seconds they organise into veins and webs that look like a living organism. Sensing ahead of every agent is one sampling call, and trail decay is one blur. Two kernels produce the emergent behaviour; the rest is bookkeeping.

The science

This is the Jones agent model of Physarum polycephalum, the slime mould that finds shortest paths through a maze and, in Nakagaki's experiment, reproduced the Tokyo rail network when food was placed at the stations. Each agent obeys three rules and holds no map: sample the trail at three points ahead, turn toward the strongest, deposit a little trail where you stand.

The trail map is the real computer. Between steps it obeys a discrete reaction–diffusion equation — a blur, a decay and a source term — which is the forward Euler step of

∂T/∂t = D∇2T − ρT + S,

with D set by the 3×3 blur and ρ by the decay slider. Deposit and diffusion spread signal outwards; decay removes it everywhere at a constant rate. The balance of the two fixes a length scale of order √(D/ρ), and that is the width of the veins you see. Raise the decay and the network becomes finer; lower it and the whole map saturates into a smear.

The patterns form because the feedback is positive and the resource is limited. An agent that follows a trail also reinforces it, so a path that attracts traffic attracts more — but decay means a trail survives only while it keeps being used. Paths compete, and the ones with less traffic fade. That is why the web appears within a few hundred steps and then coarsens: once the structure is connected, thin veins lose their traffic to thick ones and vanish, exactly as they do in the organism.

Per step: Concat the three sensor angles, Cos and Sin, Mul and Add for the sensor positions, Transpose into the sampling layout, GridSample to read the trail at all three sensors of every agent at once, Split, Greater and And to decide, Where to turn, Add a noise buffer, Mod to wrap the map, Floor, Cast, Mul and Add to build a flat integer index, Reshape and ScatterElements with reduction: "add" to deposit (ScatterND wants int64 indices, which no kernel can produce on the card), and Conv with a 3×3 kernel scaled by the decay to blur and fade. There is no random-number kernel among the 207, so eight noise buffers are uploaded once and cycled. Positions, headings and the trail never leave the card; the trail is read back once per frame to draw.
